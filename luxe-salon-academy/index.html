About Us

A Little Luxury,
Close to Home

A calm, premium salon in the heart of Indore — where expert stylists, gentle products and honest advice come together.

Luxe Salon & Academy is a boutique beauty destination on Sapna Sangeeta Road, Indore. We keep things simple: clean space, skilled hands, and services that suit real life.

From everyday haircuts to full bridal makeovers, every visit starts with a friendly consultation — so you always know what suits you before we begin.

We are also an academy: short, practical beauty courses for anyone who wants to learn makeup, hair or skin care from working professionals. (Sample description — details on call.)
