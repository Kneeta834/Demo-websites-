Our Story

A Family Craft of Celebration

Simple words about who we are and what we love to do.

Decorators who treat your big day like our own

Star Mandap Decorators and Electric Works is a Mumbai-based team for wedding and event decoration. We design mandaps, stages, lighting and flower work for weddings, receptions, sangeet nights and festivals.

From the first meeting to the last light switched off, one team handles everything — design, flowers, fabric, lights and power. You enjoy the celebration; we handle the setup.

  • ✓One team for decor + electricals — no need to call separate vendors.
  • ✓Designs for every budget — simple and elegant, or full royal style.
  • ✓On-time setup — we finish before your guests arrive.
