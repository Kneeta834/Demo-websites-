Before your visit

A few helpful answers.

How do I book an appointment?

Call +91 99834 13736 to speak with the clinic and arrange a suitable visit.

What should I bring?

Bring any previous dental records, prescriptions, X-rays, and a list of medicines you currently take, if available.

Can I call about a specific treatment?

Yes. Briefly explain your concern over the phone. The clinic can advise whether a consultation is the right next step.

What if I have severe pain or swelling?

Call the clinic promptly. If you have trouble breathing, rapidly increasing swelling, heavy bleeding, or a serious injury, seek emergency medical care immediately.

Ready to take the next step?

Call to discuss your concern and plan a visit. If email is easier, send a message with your name and preferred callback time.
